## ⚡ Post #2: Quadratic Voting & Sybil Resistance Update (v1.5.0)

**Date:** August 2026  
**Channels:** Twitter/X Thread, Medium Article, Discord Announcements  

### Announcement Text:
```
⚡ MAJOR PRODUCT UPDATE: Anti-Whale Quadratic Voting (QV) is Here! 🗳️

Based on community feedback, we've deployed Soroban Rust smart contract updates implementing Quadratic Cost Math ($N^2$).

Why does this matter?
Cost for 1 Vote = 1 Credit
Cost for 3 Votes = 9 Credits
Cost for 5 Votes = 25 Credits

This prevents high-balance wallets from dominating grant allocations and ensures community consensus wins! 🌐

Check out the updated dApp: https://frontend-eta-seven-24.vercel.app
```
