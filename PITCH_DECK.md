## Slide 2: Problem Statement
- **Centralized Gatekeeping:** Traditional scholarship awards are decided behind closed doors by opaque selection committees.
- **High Administrative Overhead:** Processing paper/PDF applications and manually disbursing funds consumes up to 25% of grant budgets.
- **Lack of Transparency & Trust:** Applicants rarely receive feedback or proof of how funds were allocated.
- **Geographic Barriers:** International students face cross-border financial friction receiving scholarship funds.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution
- **On-Chain Candidate Registration:** Students submit verified scholarship applications directly to the Soroban smart contract.
- **Community-Driven Voting:** Token holders & community members vote directly on student applications using Freighter Wallets.
- **Automated Disbursal:** Smart contracts ensure funds are allocated according to community consensus.
- **Low Gas Fees & Speed:** Built on the **Stellar Network** for sub-second settlement and micro-cent transaction fees.

---

## Slide 4: Market Opportunity
- **$100B+ Global Market:** Total annual value of global academic grants, university scholarships, and research funding.
- **Web3 Ecosystem Education:** Thousands of Web3 foundations (Stellar, Celo, Ethereum) actively search for transparent grant allocation tooling.
- **Target Audience:** Universities, DAOs, Web3 educational funds, and student grant programs worldwide.

---

## Slide 5: Technical Architecture
- **Smart Contract Layer:** Rust + Soroban SDK v25 (`initialize`, `apply_scholarship`, `approve_candidate`, `vote`, `end_voting`).
- **Frontend Layer:** React 19 + TypeScript + Vite 5.4 + Tailwind CSS with glassmorphism UI.
- **Wallet Connection:** Freighter API Integration with real-time XLM balance polling & fallback simulation mode.
- **Infrastructure:** Deployed on Stellar Testnet & Vercel Global Edge Network with CI/CD.
